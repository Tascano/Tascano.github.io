Books, primary texts (with archive.org identifiers), papers and theses, government records and census, maps, images and museums, videos, blogs and community sites, and newspapers: how to find each and how far to trust it. Reorganised from note 33 (the catalogue of notes 01-20 and its discoveries) plus every new source cited in notes 34-44.

How to use this appendix

  • Reliability: A primary document, peer-reviewed article, official record or standard scholarly edition; B reliable secondary (reputable press, museum or institution page, professional heritage writing, theses); C useful but verify (community blogs, travel sites, Wikipedia and mirrors, aggregators); D a lead only.
  • Link status (note 33, checked 18 Nov 2026): [200] page existed; [read] fetched and read; [403/blocked] exists per a search but refused automated access; [unverified] known from general knowledge, no catalogue record found (check before quoting; no link given). Sources first cited in notes 34-44 are marked new.
  • Short forms used across the book: Gaz. XIII-1, XIII-2, XIV = Gazetteer of the Bombay Presidency Thana volumes; DC = da Cunha 1876; BG = Gazetteer XIV “Bassein” entry; Hull = Bombay Mission History; LSI VII = Grierson 1905; SM = Source Material for a History of the Freedom Movement; CRIT = MMR-HCS heritage survey 2008; EI = east-indians.net parish pages; Tombo = Teixeira and Pires 2007.
  • Not reproduced here: the 656 de-duplicated URLs of note 33’s Appendix A (academic 175; community and heritage sites 125; government 42; news 237; primary texts 52; Wikipedia and mirrors 152). Use that file when you need a single URL. Gaps are marked “gap” in the tables and listed in Appendix G §4.
  • Reliability of Wikipedia and its mirrors is C throughout; use them only as pointers.

10. Reading order for a researcher

  1. Gazetteer XIV (Bassein, Sopara, Gass, Nirmal, Agashi, Arnala, Tungar) and XIII-1 (castes).
  2. Da Cunha 1876; Hull I; Tombo 1727-30; Mendiratta 2023.
  3. Grierson VII; Enthoven; BCG I.
  4. Sircar EI 32; Indraji 1882; Shah 2020; Smagur et al. 2023.
  5. SM volumes and the ICHR dictionary for 1860-1947.
  6. Census tables, CRIT, VVCMC plans for the modern city. Then close the gaps in Appendix G §4.
