Book Two

Three Towns and a Shore

The same centuries seen from the ground: Vasai, Nallasopara and Virar, each in its own right, and the coast that joins them.

Book One goes through time. This book stays in one place and looks. Vasai is the old walled city and the modern municipal town; Nallasopara is the ancient port that became a railway halt and then a suburb; Virar is the station town at the end of the line that grew by plantains and by the railway. Each has its own land, its own water, its own peoples and its own villages, and the chapters on each follow the same plan so that the three can be read side by side.

The shore between them, from Arnala’s island fort to the salt flats of the south, is a fourth place with a life of its own: the fisher-folk’s coast, which the towns have always turned their backs on and which has always fed them.
