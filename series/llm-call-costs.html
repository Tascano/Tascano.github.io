What an LLM Call Costs

or: read the receipt

The voice in my last episode cost fifty-two cents. I know because my studio prints the bill before it spends anything: 29 lines, 5,219 characters, $0.52. Most people using AI only see the bill at the end of the month. So this episode prints one.

The short version

  • Two meters. Every call is billed for tokens in (instructions, files, the whole chat so far) and tokens out (the answer), at different rates. A rough rule: 1,000 tokens is about 750 English words, and every model counts its own way.
  • Output costs about five times input. On Anthropic's list, Claude Opus 5.5 is $4 per million tokens in and $20 out; OpenAI's flagship is $10 in and $50 out (November 2026). The prompt is read in one pass; the answer is written one token at a time.
  • In one call, the answer is the bill. 2,000 tokens in and 800 out comes to 2.4 cents: the answer is 29% of the tokens and 67% of the bill.
  • In an agent, re-reading is the bill. Every turn re-sends the whole conversation. Thirty turns of a modest agent sends 1.17 million tokens in and only 15,000 out: $4.98, with re-reading at 94%.
  • Pay less. Cache what repeats (a cache hit on Opus 5.5 is $0.20 per million, a twentieth: the same session drops to $0.86), batch what can wait (50% off at both labs), send easy work to a small model (Haiku 5.5 writes for a fortieth of Opus), ask for shorter answers, and log tokens per feature.
One call: 2,000 × $4/M + 800 × $20/M = $0.008 + $0.016 = $0.024
Agent, 30 turns (10,000 tokens to start, +2,000 a turn, 500 out a turn): 1,170,000 in = $4.68 · 15,000 out = $0.30 · total $4.98
Cached: 1,102,000 hits × $0.20/M + 68,000 writes × $5/M + $0.30 = $0.86 (−83%)

The call and the agent session are illustrative arithmetic on Anthropic's November 2026 list prices for Claude Opus 5.5; prices change, so check the live pages. The $0.52 is my studio's real estimate for episode 6's narration.
