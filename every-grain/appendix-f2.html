0. How to read this file

Link status (checked with curl on 18 Nov 2026 from this environment): - [200] the URL returned HTTP 200 (page exists; content not necessarily read in full). - [read] I fetched or searched it and the metadata below comes from the record itself (archive.org metadata API, search result text). - [403/blocked] server refused automated access; the record exists per a search result but could not be opened here. - [unverified, memory] I know the work from general knowledge, but found no catalogue record in this pass. Check the citation before quoting it. No link is given unless verified.

Reliability (A = best): - A primary document, peer-reviewed, official record or standard scholarly edition. - B reliable secondary: reputable press, museum or institutional page, professional heritage writing, theses. - C useful but verify: community blogs, travel sites, fandom wikis, Wikipedia and its mirrors, aggregators, commercial sites. - D treat as a lead only: anonymous, unsourced or promotional material.

Known conflicts flagged in the notes (do not resolve silently): the date of the Portuguese cession (1533 Gazetteer vs 23 Dec 1534 vs 1535); the siege dates of 1739 (17 Feb start; 4 May white flag vs 16 May capture; 23 May evacuation; the 4 May and 16 May dates are in fact one event in the Old Style and New Style calendars, 11 days apart, per Kelkar’s Peshwa Daftar dates in note 63; the 23 May evacuation and the 23 May flag-raising may be two different days); whether the Bhuigaon fragment is Rock Edict IX (Sircar, Epigraphia Indica 32) or XI (Wikipedia); Francis D’Britto’s birth year (1942 or 1943).

Part A is a harvest, not a re-verification. Reliability there is rated by source class. Appendix A lists all 656 unique URLs.
