Every unresolved conflict and gap flagged in the research notes (01-44), grouped so that each can become a task. Where a section of the book says “no data here”, this appendix says what would fill it.

How to use this appendix

  • Conflict = two or more sources disagree and the notes did not settle it. Gap = nothing found. Unsourced = a claim repeated without a source chain. Family = a question that families or parish members could help answer (oral history, papers at home, parish registers).
  • Priority: A (changes a statement in the book), B (adds detail), C (colour). Citations [nNN].
  • Most gaps are search gaps (the notes could not reach a source), not proof of absence; where the notes say so it is repeated.

7. Questions families could help answer

  1. Parish and family papers: baptism and marriage certificates, old foral/land documents, wills; which parish a family belonged to before the village’s own parish (Gass before 1942; Bhuigaon before 1946).
  2. Surname stories: which godfather or landlord gave the family name; whether a family kept a Marathi name (Naik, Patil, Mhatre); any memory of Toscano/Tuscano ancestry.
  3. Village names and hamlets: names of adas and padas, wells, ponds and crosses (khursa) and their feast days; what “Gomes Ali” and “Rumao Ali” mean locally.
  4. Gass: where the two ancient stones (Badhuya; Kodasa Kalavadasa) are now; where “Sonarbhat”, “Mala” and the Taki Pada finds are; the reason the parish was founded in 1942; the first church builder; the 1957 works; the Gass landing’s last use; memory of the saint’s “birth” claim; Sircar’s location.
  5. Language: recordings of grandparents (Kadodi, Vadvali, East Indian); songs, proverbs, riddles; lullabies and wedding songs (Hadisoli, Lagin, Varad); Portuguese-derived words in the home.
  6. Dress and objects: photographs of red caps and lal lugda; jewellery; houses and crosses on doors.
  7. Work: betel and banana gardens, wells (bhowkal), sukeli making, toddy and salt memories; when boats stopped coming to Gass and Bolinj.
  8. Migration: when relatives left for Bombay, the Gulf, Canada, Australia; why.
  9. Freedom struggle and civic memory: anyone who recalls Bal Samant and the 9 Sept 1942 firing at Chowk Bazar; relatives who were in the 1930 prohibition work at Sopara or the 1921 Bassein conference.
  10. Festivals: the Pali Fiesta, Remedy feast, Nirmal yatra stalls, Aagera; first-fruit blessings.
  11. Origin traditions: whether the Orissa-entertainer story is told in the family and by whom; whether any families recall pre-Portuguese caste names (Samvedi, Palshe).
  12. Land loss: stories of fields sold, flooded or built over (Achole, Tulinj, Giriz), and the 2025 Gass dumping-ground objections.
