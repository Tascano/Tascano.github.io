A directory of every named person in the research notes, grouped by era and then by field.

How to use this directory

  • Dates are as the notes give them. Where sources disagree, both readings are shown (“1556/57”).
  • Status tells you how firm the person is:
    • DOC: documented by an inscription, a text or a record.
    • TRAD: local or community tradition.
    • LEGEND: a literary or religious legend with no independent evidence.
    • ALLEGATION: a criminal or civil allegation with no conviction stated in the notes. Where the notes report a court outcome, it is given.
  • Citations follow the book’s style: note number, then a short source, e.g. <span class="cite">[n25; TG-XIV]</span>. Short forms:
    • TG-XIV: Bombay Gazetteer vol. XIV, Thana: Places of Interest (1882).
    • TG-I and TG-II: vol. XIII parts I and II (1882).
    • DC: da Cunha, Chaul and Bassein (1876).
    • BG: TG-XIV’s “Bassein” entry as read in note 32.
    • EI: Epigraphia Indica.
    • WP: Wikipedia.
    • FPJ: Free Press Journal.
  • The village is spelled Gass throughout, whatever spelling the source uses. Vasai is a Diocese, not an Archdiocese.
  • People who are only named as authors of a cited work, with no other connection to the area, are listed in §11 (scholars) only when the notes discuss their work on this coast.
